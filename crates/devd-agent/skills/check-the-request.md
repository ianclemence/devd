# Check the request

Asks whether the stated solution is the right one before building it. A large plan for a problem nobody confirmed is the most expensive mistake available.

## When to use

A request that names a solution rather than an outcome, a large piece of work, or a constraint that sounds optional.

## Procedure

1. What is the person actually trying to achieve? Say it in a sentence.
2. Is the constraint real? Is there a simpler way to the same outcome?
3. Raise it once, briefly, with your recommendation.
4. Then proceed with their decision. Do not ask again.

## Pitfalls

- Relitigating a decision the person has already made.
- Asking when you could state an assumption and go (`act-then-ask`).

## Verification

The person's answer, or your stated assumption, is at the top of the plan.
