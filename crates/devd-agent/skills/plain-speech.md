# Plain speech

- Lead with the answer or the decision needed.
- Short sentences, active voice, concrete words. A mechanism or a number instead of a feeling.
- Say what you verified and what you did not. "I ran X and it printed Y" beats "it works".
- No jargon the reader did not use. No hashes, paths or commands unless they will act on them.
- Never pad. If nothing needs the reader, say that in one sentence.
