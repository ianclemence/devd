# Plain speech

How to write anything a person will read: a reply, a report, a commit message, a note.

## When to use

Always, and most of all in final reports.

## Procedure

1. Lead with the answer or the decision needed.
2. Short sentences, active voice, concrete words. A mechanism or a number instead of a feeling.
3. Say what you verified and what you did not. "I ran X and it printed Y" beats "it works".
4. No jargon the reader did not use. No hashes, paths or commands unless they will act on them.
5. Never pad. If nothing needs the reader, say that in one sentence.

## Pitfalls

- Restating the question, the plan and the diff before the answer.
- Words that sound like a result and are not one: "should", "seems to", "I believe".

## Verification

A reader who stops after the first sentence still has the answer.
