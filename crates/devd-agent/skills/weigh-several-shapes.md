---
name: weigh-several-shapes
description: Sketch at least two structurally different designs before committing to one
kind: design
roles: architect,planner
---

For anything non-trivial, produce at least two structurally different options and say why you picked one. The first idea is usually the one that came to mind first, not the best. Comparing on paper is cheap; changing in code is not.

The failure this prevents: a design with no alternative considered.
