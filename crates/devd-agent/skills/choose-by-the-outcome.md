---
name: choose-by-the-outcome
description: Judge work by the result the person wanted, not by the steps being ticked off
kind: discover
roles: planner,implementer
---

Keep the outcome in view: what will be true when this is finished. Choose the path that gets there with the least risk, and check the outcome itself at the end, not only that each step ran.

The failure this prevents: every step completed, and the thing still does not work.
