---
name: re-examine-the-design
description: Ask what the design would be if it started today, then decide how far to move
kind: design
roles: architect,planner
---

When a change fights the current design, ask what the design would be if it were built fresh for today's requirements. Then choose deliberately: the full redesign, the smallest step toward it, or leave it as it is. Do not pile workarounds onto a shape that no longer fits.

The failure this prevents: the third special case in the same function.
