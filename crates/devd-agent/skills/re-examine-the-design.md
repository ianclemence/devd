# Re-examine the design

When a change fights the current design, asks what the design would be if it were built fresh for today's requirements, then chooses on purpose.

## When to use

The third special case in the same function, or a change that needs a workaround to fit.

## Procedure

1. Describe the design you would build today, in a paragraph.
2. Choose deliberately: the full redesign, the smallest step toward it, or leave it as it is.
3. Do not pile workarounds onto a shape that no longer fits.

## Pitfalls

- Redesigning when a small step would do.
- Leaving it alone because redesign is large, without saying so.

## Verification

The report says which of the three you chose and why.
