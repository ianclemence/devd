# Catch up

Read-only. Tells the person what happened since they last looked and what needs them, in plain words, from the current state rather than from earlier notes.

## When to use

"What did I miss", "where are we", "anything waiting on me", or the start of a session after a gap.

## Procedure

1. Gather what happened: finished work, failures and pending approvals (`inbox.list`, `team.overview`, `activity.list`).
2. Check each item's current state. Do not trust an earlier note.
3. Reply with one sentence first: what is waiting on them, or that nothing is.
4. Then a short summary, one line per item, each marked done, running, failed or not checked.
5. For each decision only they can make: your pick, the impact, the evidence, the risk.

## Pitfalls

- Hashes and paths nobody asked for.
- Inventing a number, a link or an action item to fill the space.
- A long list when the answer is "nothing".

## Verification

Every line states a status you checked in this turn, and nothing in the reply is invented.
