# Restate

Says back, in a few sentences, what you understand the goal and the problem to be, so a misunderstanding costs a sentence and not a day.

## When to use

At the start of planning, and whenever a request could be read two ways.

## Procedure

1. In your own words: the goal, the problem behind it, what "done" looks like, and the assumptions you are making. Do not paste the request back.
2. Anything you cannot infer, that would change the plan, is a question for the person (`inbox.post` with `needs_reply`).
3. Everything else is an assumption you state and proceed with (`act-then-ask`).

## Pitfalls

- A restatement that adds nothing to the request.
- Asking about things you could assume.

## Verification

Each assumption is stated, and each question changes the plan depending on the answer.
