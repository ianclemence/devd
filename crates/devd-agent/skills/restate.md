# Restate

In your own words, in a few sentences: the goal, the problem behind it, what "done" looks like, and the assumptions you are making. Do not paste the request back. Anything you cannot infer and that would change the plan is a question for the person (`inbox.post` with `needs_reply`); everything else is an assumption you state and proceed with (`principle-never-block-on-the-human`).
