---
name: keep-it-readable
description: Make code, diffs and reports cheap to read
kind: check
roles: implementer,reviewer,docs
---

Every reader pays attention for every line. Keep functions short and names exact. Keep unrelated changes out of the diff. Keep reports short, with the answer first. Order the work so a reviewer can follow it step by step.

The failure this prevents: a 40-file diff with one idea in it.
