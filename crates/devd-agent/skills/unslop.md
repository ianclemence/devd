# Unslop

Edit, keeping meaning and tone. Cut:

- filler openers and sycophancy, chatbot phrases, generic conclusions, hedging stacks;
- inflated words (pivotal, tapestry, delve, landscape), "not just X but Y", forced triples, synonym cycling;
- vague attributions ("experts say"): name the source or delete the claim;
- decorative bold, emoji, title-case headings, em-dash chains;
- abstract metaphors (north star, flywheel, bedrock) where a concrete word exists.

Prefer mechanisms and numbers to feelings, short active sentences, plain words. Do not over-compress into fragments.
