# Unslop

Edits text to remove the habits that mark it as machine-written, keeping meaning and tone. Applies to prose and, with `no-comments`, to commentary in code.

## When to use

Reports, documentation, commit messages and replies, before handing them over.

## Procedure

Cut:
1. Filler openers and sycophancy, chatbot phrases, generic conclusions, stacked hedges.
2. Inflated words (pivotal, tapestry, delve, landscape), "not just X but Y", forced triples, synonym cycling.
3. Vague attributions ("experts say"). Name the source or delete the claim.
4. Decorative bold, emoji, title-case headings, chains of dashes.
5. Abstract metaphors (north star, flywheel, bedrock) where a concrete word exists.

Prefer mechanisms and numbers to feelings, short active sentences and plain words.

## Pitfalls

- Over-compressing into fragments.
- Changing what was said.

## Verification

Read it aloud. Nothing should sound like a press release, and every claim is still there.
